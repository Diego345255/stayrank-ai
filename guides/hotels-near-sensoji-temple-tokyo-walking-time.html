The straight-line distance problem

Most listings compute "distance to landmark" as a straight line between two coordinates. That number ignores rivers, elevated roads, and the actual street grid - so a hotel that's "0.3 miles" away in a straight line can be a 20-minute walk once you account for the nearest bridge or crossing.

How StayRank AI computes it instead

Enter a landmark (like "Senso-ji Temple") and StayRank AI calls OSRM - a real, open-source street-network routing engine - to compute an actual walking route and time for nearby hotels, the same kind of routing a maps app would give you turn-by-turn, not a geometry-only estimate.
