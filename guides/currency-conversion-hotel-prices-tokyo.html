Real live currency conversion on Tokyo hotel prices

A fixed "approximate conversion" that was accurate the day someone hardcoded it drifts further from reality every week exchange rates move. StayRank AI pulls a real, live daily rate instead - here's exactly where to see it.

Where the rate actually comes from

Live daily exchange rates from the European Central Bank, via the free Frankfurter API - the same authoritative source used by financial and government sites, not a third-party estimate or a rate frozen at build time.

How to see it for a Tokyo trip

Currency conversion lives inside Trip Planner Pro's trip budget panel, one step past the hotel search - here's the honest path to it, not a one-click shortcut that doesn't actually exist:

  1. 1 Search Tokyo and open the Trip Planner Pro section below your ranked hotels.
  2. 2 Click "Build my trip plan" for your dates.
  3. 3 In the Trip budget panel, enter your home currency's 3-letter code (e.g. USD, GBP, EUR) - the total updates using that day's real ECB rate.

Common questions

Is the rate updated in real time?

It reflects the ECB's own daily published rate, not an intraday feed - accurate for planning, though it can drift slightly by the moment you actually pay.

Does this affect the price shown on the hotel list itself?

No - the main ranked list shows the hotel's listed price in its original currency; the converted total appears specifically in the trip budget panel once you've built a trip plan.
