How to search hotels by describing your trip in plain English

Instead of stacking a dozen filters one at a time, you can just write a sentence - "quiet hotel near a subway station, not in a loud nightlife area, rooms that don't feel tiny" - and StayRank AI turns that into a real ranking, not a keyword-matching gimmick.

What actually happens to your sentence

Two real mechanisms run on your free-text description, not one black box:

AI intent parsing - when configured, an LLM reads your description and turns phrases like "quiet is essential" into an actual weight increase on the quiet metric, on a continuous scale, rather than a fixed keyword bump.

Real text-match scoring - independently, a TF-IDF cosine-similarity comparison checks your exact wording against each hotel's real neighborhood description, tags, strengths, and review text, and blends that into the final score at a fixed 20% weight. This runs regardless of whether the AI parser is available.

What happens if the AI parser fails

Every LLM call in StayRank AI is wrapped in error handling that falls back to a rule-based keyword parser automatically - no key configured, a rate limit, a timeout, or a malformed response all land on the same fallback path, on the same request. You're never shown an error for it, and the text-match scoring keeps running either way, since it doesn't depend on the LLM at all.

Common questions

What happens if the AI parser is unavailable?

The search doesn't fail - it falls back to a rule-based keyword and TF-IDF parser automatically, with no visible error.

Does writing more detail actually help?

Generally yes for the text-match component - more specific wording gives the real TF-IDF comparison more to match against each hotel's actual data.
