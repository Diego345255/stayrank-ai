Common questions

Is room size a real per-room measurement?

It's a computed metric score, not a live square-footage measurement - no honest source publishes that per hotel, so it's disclosed as an estimate rather than presented as a fact.

Does the Family persona filter out hotels without kid amenities?

No - it re-ranks the same real hotel data rather than excluding anything. A hotel with a lower family-fit score can still appear, just ranked lower for this traveler type.
