Why this beats a typical booking site

  • No fake urgency. Never "Only 1 room left!" - UK/EU regulators have flagged that exact practice on major OTAs since 2024.
  • Real reviews only. When live data is on, every review comes straight from Booking.com, unedited - nothing purchased, filtered, or fabricated.
  • Zero ads. No sponsored placements bend the ranking - every hotel is scored by the same model you can inspect below.
  • No notification spam. No push notifications, no re-engagement emails, ever.
  • Transparent AI. Every match reason is grounded in the hotel's actual data, not a black-box score.
  • All-in pricing. The nightly price shown is the price - no fees revealed only at checkout.
  • Accessibility, only when confirmed. The ♿ Accessible filter only shows hotels with a specifically reported wheelchair-accessible room or facility (real Hotelbeds data) - we never treat "not reported" as "not accessible," unlike listings travelers have reported as advertising accessibility they didn't actually have.
  • No AI review smoothing. "What guests actually mention" counts real keyword mentions across a hotel's own unedited reviews - never an AI-generated summary, which a July 2026 consumer-watchdog investigation found could soften or omit real complaints like food poisoning and hygiene failures on a major review site.

Trip Planner Pro

Weather, flights, and a full itinerary - not just hotels

✓ Pro active

Trip Planner Pro turns your ranked hotel into a full day-by-day plan: a real weather forecast, landscape/culture/entertainment picks near your hotel, and a flight time + fare estimate from wherever you're starting.

  • Real weather, not a guess - pulled live from Open-Meteo for your actual trip dates.
  • Real places - nature, culture, nightlife, and family spots sourced from OpenStreetMap, not invented.
  • Honest flight estimates - distance-based time/fare ranges, clearly labeled as estimates, never presented as a live quote.

Prototype note: this "unlock" is a local demo toggle - no account, no payment method, nothing is charged.
